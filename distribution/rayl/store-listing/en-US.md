# Rayl

**Short description:** Lightning payments tailored to your Blink, NWC, or Spark wallet.

Connect your Blink account with an API key, connect a compatible wallet with an
NWC connection link, or import your Spark wallet with its recovery phrase.
Scan QR codes and pay Lightning invoices, Lightning addresses, and LNURLs with your wallet’s own payment experience.

Keep local payment shortcuts and groups in the Hub. Customize confirmation,
currency display, language, and theme. The Blink experience also includes
automatic contact import and funding-wallet selection.

Rayl connects to one wallet at a time. Service purchases are not available in this
version. Blip, Lasr, and Flint remain purpose-built
alternatives. This prerelease listing requires owner review before publication.
