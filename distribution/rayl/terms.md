## Payments, fees, and Auto Pay

- **Review payment details carefully:** Verify the recipient, amount, display
  currency, funding wallet, and fees before sending a payment. Display-currency
  conversions are estimates based on exchange-rate data and are not guaranteed
  quotes.
- **Auto Pay:** Depending on your confirmation settings, scanning or opening a
  payment request may submit a payment automatically without a separate
  confirmation sheet when it matches your configured rules. Choose "Always ask
  for confirmation" in Settings if you want to confirm every payment manually.
- **Finality and uncertain outcomes:** Lightning payments are generally final
  and irreversible. A network timeout or uncertain status does not prove that a
  payment failed; check your wallet provider before retrying to avoid duplicate
  payments. Closing the app or removing a wallet connection in Rayl does not
  cancel an in-flight payment or revoke credentials at your wallet provider.
